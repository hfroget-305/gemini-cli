## 2025-05-22 - [UX improvement] Add missing keyboard shortcuts to Help display
**Learning:** Users often rely on the in-app help (/? or /help) to discover features. While the CLI supports many powerful readline-style shortcuts (Ctrl+A/E, Ctrl+R, etc.), these were not documented in the UI, leading to lower discoverability of efficient navigation and history management.
**Action:** Always ensure that common keyboard shortcuts supported by the underlying text buffer or system are reflected in the help menu to empower power users.
